# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and focus on technical outcomes.
- Provide actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or doxxing.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to:
- `conduct@smainer.io`
