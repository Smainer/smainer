# Code of Conduct

*We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.*

</div>

---

## Our Commitment

All participants in the Smainer community — contributors, maintainers, and users — deserve a safe and productive environment. We pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
- Assume good intent and focus on technical outcomes.
- Provide actionable, specific, and kind feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or doxxing.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a permanent ban.

---

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **[conduct@smainer.io](mailto:conduct@smainer.io)**.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality.
